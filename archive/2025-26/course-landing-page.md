# USC Introduction to Logic

The textbook for this course [Introduction to Logic](https://logic.gabrieluzquiano.org/) is available online. You may access sections of the book directly from the online platform [USC Logic Web](https://dornsife.usc.edu/usclogicweb), which we will use as a companion to the course.

Assignments will be visible in your [user page](https://carnap.io/user/) on Carnap. You will find a variety of practice problems (and solutions) for different parts of the course in the online platform  [USC Logic Web](https://dornsife.usc.edu/usclogicweb).

Below you will find course materials and a schedule for the course, which includes links to lecture notes and assignments for the course.
